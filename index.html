Mastery of the Leaf: Tobacco Varieties

Virginia (Flue-Cured)

Known as "Brightleaf," this variety is cured in heated barns, resulting in a golden-yellow color. It boasts a naturally high sugar content, delivering a light, bright, and subtly sweet flavor profile.

Burley (Air-Cured)

Air-cured in open barns for several weeks, Burley leaves turn a rich brown. With virtually no natural sugar, it offers a robust, earthy, and deep cocoa-like character that absorbs flavorings exceptionally well.

Oriental (Sun-Cured)

Grown in the dry climates of the Mediterranean and sun-cured. These small leaves are packed with natural oils, offering a highly aromatic, spicy, and fragrant smoke that adds complexity to any premium blend.

The American Blend

The industry gold standard, meticulously combining the sweetness of Virginia, the depth of Burley, and the spice of Oriental tobaccos to create a perfectly balanced, full-flavored cigarette experience.
